Tutorial 4: PHY121

1. A proton at speed v = 5.00 × 10^6 m/s orbits at radius r = 200 cm outside
a charged sphere. Find the sphere's charge.

2. A cube has sides of length L and sits on the coordinate axes. The electric
field in the region is E = (a + bx)i^ + cj^. Find the electric flux through
each of the six cube faces and the total electric flux.

3. The electric potential of some configuration is given by the expression

   V(r) = A * (e^(-λr) / r) + B/r,

where A, B, and C are constants. Find the electric field E(r), the charge
density ρ(r), and the total charge Q.

4. Find the energy stored in a uniformly charged solid sphere of radius R
and charge q. Do it in three different ways:

   (i)  Use W = (1/2) ∫ ρV dτ. Find the potential first.
   (ii) Use W = (ε₀/2) ∫ E² dτ. Don't forget to integrate over all space.

5. Find the electric field inside and outside a sphere that carries a charge
density of the form ρ(r) = a + br − cr^3, for some arbitrary constants a, b.

6. A long, straight wire has length L and a circular cross-section with area
πa². Arrange two such wires so they are parallel and separated by a
distance d. You may assume that L >> d >> a and ignore end effects.

   (i)  Graph the electrostatic potential along the straight line that connects
        the center of one wire to the center of the other wire for the case when
        the two wires have equal and opposite charge per unit length.
   (ii) Derive an approximate expression for the capacitance of this two-
        conductor system.

7. Find the potential due to a uniformly charged solid sphere of radius R
and charge q at points: (i) inside  (ii) on the surface and (iii) outside the
sphere. Also plot how the potential varies across the distance.

8. Find the potential of a uniformly charged spherical shell of radius R.
